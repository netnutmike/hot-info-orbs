XIAO orb power

The XIAO ESP32-S3 orb draws about 11% less than the SuperMini prototype, and its power-on surge is about two-thirds of the SuperMini's. Five GC9A01 displays, firmware 1.8.0, on Wi-Fi, fed 5.000 V from a Nordic PPK II.

Thermal camera, default settings, fed on the 5V pin, which is the same point as USB-C's 5 V on the XIAO. The XIAO settles about 11 °C over the room, where the SuperMini ran about 19 °C over. That fits the XIAO's buck converter (SGM6029) taking the place of the SuperMini's linear regulator, but the shots do not prove the cause. Compare the rise, not the reading: the rooms differed. The heat sits on the shield can over the ESP32-S3, and the converter shows no hot spot of its own. The display backs cannot be shot on the XIAO board; the SuperMini board left them in view. White and red are hottest, blue coolest.

For the power supply

The advice does not change: use a 2 A supply. It covers the XIAO's 781 mA surge with plenty of room, and it still covers the SuperMini's 1.15 A. A 1 A supply would probably start a XIAO orb, but 2 A costs nothing extra.

Read these before quoting the numbers

!Screens off is not established as backlight. Off sleeps all five display controllers, yet the orb still draws 1.33 W. The likely reason is that the GC9A01 boards wire the backlight straight to power, but nobody looked at the screens during the off window.
!A 2.07 A inrush reading was thrown out. That capture kept only 1.3 s of a 3.5 s stream. Dropped bytes misalign the PPK's 4-byte samples and decode as garbage. The four complete captures agree within 12 mA.
  • Board: XIAO carrier with a plain XIAO ESP32-S3 (not the Plus), firmware 1.8.0 release.
  • Feed: PPK II VOUT soldered to the XIAO's 5V pin, USB-C unplugged. The SuperMini was most likely fed the same way; that was not recorded at the time.
  • Instrument: PPK II source meter at 5.000 V, driven from a script at its native 100 kSa/s, every window checked for a full sample count.
  • Dim and off were switched over the orb's settings page; each window started at least 60 s after the restart that a save causes. All settings were put back afterwards, except Stocks, which was left on.
  • Not measured: the XIAO ESP32-S3 Plus, and the display backs on the XIAO board (covered by the board).
